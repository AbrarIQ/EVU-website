What is EVU?

EVU is an AI-native venture studio and software application development subsidiary of Thinking Craft, Inc. EVU builds and operates AI-powered SaaS applications for small and medium businesses across roughly 28 ventures, sharing engineering talent, infrastructure, and workflows across the portfolio instead of building each company from scratch.

How EVU structures its portfolio

EVU organizes its ventures into six clusters, sharing build resources and IP across all of them:

AI & Creative Tools

  • Selfware — Cognitive profiling and personalization engine
  • VFXB — AI video creation and conversational editing
  • Twin Brain — Cognitive AI research platform
  • Cheaper — AI comparison shopping engine
  • Realidream — AI-powered augmented reality

Marketplaces & Social

  • Elaview — Marketplace for physical advertising space
  • Unhire — Premium, rapid-response freelancer marketplace
  • Hyperbuds — AI creator collaboration platform
  • Cyberly — Virtual expert consultation platform
  • Cyberkismet — AI-powered dating platform
  • Spagram — Wellness services marketplace
  • Evercove — Sustainability marketplace

Fintech & Investment

  • Glix — Multi-currency fintech, blockchain-based
  • StockTool — AI investment research platform
  • SalesFam — Sales tracking and commission management

Estate & Life Planning

  • SuperWill — Estate planning automation
  • Unspouse — Divorce automation platform

Branding, Web & Domains

  • Feelize — Branding and digital product studio

Infrastructure & Emerging Ventures

  • Proxina — Navigation technology for the visually impaired

EVU's relationship to Thinking Craft

Thinking Craft, Inc. is the parent company and owns the core intellectual property. EVU is its wholly-owned software application development subsidiary — the build engine that develops and operates the application portfolio across every venture listed above.

How the studio model works

EVU builds multiple ventures in parallel on shared tools, talent, and an AI-native development workflow. Each build refines the workflow stack for the next, compressing MVP cost and timeline relative to a traditional single-company build. EVU retains majority equity in each venture; founders and developers earn equity through vesting.

A message from Mark Knobel, Founder & CEO:

As I reflect on the generational landscape shift caused by artificial intelligence, I'm reminded of the irreplaceable value of the human mind. Evu was founded by software application developers with the moxie to create innovations that don't yet exist, specifically targeting the intersection of human needs and technological possibility. We build with genuine passion.

As professional 'digital farmers,' we cultivate solutions rather than merely build products. This agricultural metaphor reflects our nurturing approach to innovation—planting ideas, providing the right environment for growth, and harvesting only when they're ready to provide maximum value. We reshape processes to maximize ease-of-use because complexity is the enemy of adoption.

While most of what we build leverages AI and machine learning, there remains a distinctively human component to everything we create. The who, what, and why of Evu's idea engine stem from genuine human insights: we build for real people with real needs, create solutions that address meaningful problems, and are driven by the purpose of making technology serve humanity rather than the reverse.

We envision a better world for humans, by humans—one that's more connected, seamless, sustainable, and democratized. This vision guides our daily decisions, from breaking down barriers between people and systems to creating intuitive experiences that feel like natural extensions of human capability.

Our nimble team of data engineers, scientists, and inventors works collaboratively to accelerate powerful ideas into industry-shaping applications and marketplaces. As AI continues transforming our world, Evu remains committed to ensuring that humanity leads the way—harnessing technological power with human wisdom and compassion.
